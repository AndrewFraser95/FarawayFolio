- **Product**: "Faraway Folio, Volume One" — a curated set of the best generated scenes, delivered as high-res digital downloads (print-ready + phone wallpaper crops).

Realistic only with actual traffic — this product's job is to convert whatever audience the content builds, not to be the sole growth engine. Should be live and linked from the site/bio well before waiting on affiliate approval or follower count to grow.

### 2. Amazon Associates
Apply once the site has some real traffic (a few weeks of consistent posting). Needs 3 qualifying sales within 180 days to stay approved, so this only starts paying once there's an actual audience clicking through — not a week-one lever.

### 3. LTK / ShopMy
Usually gate on engagement/follower thresholds for brand-new accounts. Apply once IG has a consistent posting history to point to.
