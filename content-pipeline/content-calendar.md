# Faraway Folio — first 2 weeks

## Posting rhythm
- Instagram: 1 feed post/day, 3-4 Stories/day, 2 Reels/week (Reels drive most reach on a new account)
- X: 2-3 posts/day (low effort — repurpose IG captions + a link back to the site)

## ComfyUI prompt themes (rotate to avoid a repetitive feed)
1. Balcony / terrace, golden hour, linen curtains, coastal town in the distance
2. Boutique hotel room, warm neutral tones, morning light, open suitcase styled aesthetically
3. Cafe table, espresso, passport + sunglasses, cobblestone street blurred behind
4. Poolside, quiet luxury resort, folded towel, single potted palm
5. Train platform / vintage departure board, soft film grain, traveler silhouette
6. Market street, woven bags, warm afternoon light, textiles

Keep a consistent color grade (warm neutrals, muted golds, soft contrast) across all of these — the aesthetic consistency is what makes an AI-content account read as a "brand" rather than random images.

## Monetization sequencing (fastest to slowest)
1. **Digital product (fastest, no approval needed):** a "quiet luxury" Lightroom/mobile preset pack or a curated packing-list PDF sold via Gumroad/Payhip — can launch in week 1, before any affiliate program approves you.
2. **Amazon Associates:** apply once the site is live with the picks pages populated — approval typically needs 3 qualifying sales within 180 days, so apply early but don't expect income here immediately.
3. **LTK (LikeToKnow.it) / ShopMy:** apply once IG has consistent posting history (most require an active audience/engagement, not just follower count) — good fit for outfit/travel-gear tagging directly in posts.
4. **Direct brand affiliate links** (luggage brands, travel gear) once you have engagement data to show them.

Given the £2k target, a bundled digital product (presets/packing guide) sold directly to your own audience is realistically the fastest lever — affiliate commissions from a brand-new account take months to compound.
